More than a box.
It's a story

Shumen Box is a physical box filled with carefully selected products from local producers.
But the real value lies beyond the box.
Each product includes a QR code linking to a Digital Produce Passport — an online profile providing information about the producer, the process, the ingredients, and the passion behind the product.

One scan. One story. Total transparency.

Our first prototype

Our first prototype brought the Shumen Box concept to life. Designed to showcase local products and connect them with a digital experience, it demonstrates how tradition and innovation can work together.
